SUPPORT

How can we help?

Get help with purchases, report a question that looks wrong, or ask about privacy.

Restore a purchase

  1. Open Oathwise.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought with.
  4. If access is still missing, open a support request with the product name and what happened.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions and choose Oathwise.

A question or answer looks wrong

Tell us the question text and the country and test version you study for. For U.S. questions about current officials, we follow the answers published at uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.

Open a support request

Privacy request

Open a support request and choose Privacy. Do not include receipts, immigration documents or other personal information in a public issue. We will offer a private follow-up when needed.
