Privacy Policy

Oathwise is a study app. It does not need to know who you are, and it is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains the data used by the Oathwise iOS app.

1. Who operates Oathwise

Oathwise is operated by Nghia Le (“Oathwise,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). For privacy questions or requests, use our Support page.

2. Data stored on your device

Your choices and progress are stored only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone: the country and test version you study for, your test date, your readiness score, mock test results, and whether you have finished onboarding or used the free mock test. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Purchases

Apple processes all payments. We use RevenueCat to show purchase options, confirm purchases, and restore access on your devices. RevenueCat receives purchase and transaction information from Apple and a random identifier created for your app installation, along with basic device information such as the app version and iOS version. Oathwise does not create an account, so RevenueCat does not receive your name or email address from us. We do not enable RevenueCat’s collection of advertising identifiers. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising and do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and the app does not ask for tracking permission.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not ask for your immigration status, visa details, documents, photos, contacts or location.
  • Oathwise has no AI features and does not send your answers to any AI service.

5. Ratings

Oathwise may ask whether you want to rate the app using Apple’s standard rating prompt. Apple handles that prompt and any rating you give.

6. Children

Oathwise is intended for adults preparing for a citizenship test. It is not directed at children.

7. Service providers

  • Apple — App Store distribution, payments and platform services.
  • RevenueCat — purchase confirmation, restoration and subscription status.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting this website. This site has no analytics script or advertising cookie.

8. Your choices

You can delete all data stored by Oathwise by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us through the Support page.

9. Changes

We will update this page if the app starts to handle data differently, and will change the effective date above.

10. Contact

Use the Support page. Do not post receipts or personal documents in a public GitHub issue.
